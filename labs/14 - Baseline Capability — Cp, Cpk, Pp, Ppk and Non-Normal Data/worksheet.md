### 6. Step notes

**Step 1.** Recap capability in full: Cp = (USL - LSL) / 6 sigma measures spread only; Cpk also accounts for centring and is always <= Cp.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 2.** Recap the acceptance benchmarks: Cpk >= 1.33 capable, >= 1.67 for critical characteristics, and the relationship between Cpk and sigma level.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 3.** Learn the distinction most practitioners get wrong: Cp/Cpk use WITHIN-subgroup (short-term) variation; Pp/Ppk use TOTAL (long-term) variation.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 4.** Understand the consequence: reporting Cpk on an unstable process flatters it, because between-subgroup drift is excluded from the estimate.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 5.** Establish the rule — the process must be STABLE before capability means anything. Check stability with a control chart first.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 6.** Test your capstone Y for normality using Anderson-Darling or Shapiro-Wilk. Read the p-value: p < 0.05 rejects normality.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 7.** Recognise that cycle time, waiting time and defect counts are routinely non-normal and usually right-skewed — this is expected, not an error.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 8.** For non-normal data, choose your route: apply a Box-Cox or Johnson transformation, or fit the correct distribution (Weibull, lognormal, exponential) directly.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 9.** Apply the transformation, re-test normality, then compute capability on the transformed scale — and report results in the ORIGINAL units.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 10.** Calculate both Cpk and Ppk for your capstone Y and explain the gap between them. A large gap means the process drifts between subgroups.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 11.** Record the baseline capability in your capstone pack alongside the Lab 3 sigma level.

____________________________________________________________________________________
