### 6. Step notes

**Step 1.** Recap the data hierarchy in full: nominal, ordinal, interval and ratio — and why the type determines which statistical test is legal.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 2.** Recap continuous versus discrete (attribute) data, and why continuous data needs far smaller samples to reach the same confidence.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 3.** List every metric your capstone requires: the project Y, each sub-Y from your flowdown, and each candidate X you intend to test.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 4.** Classify each metric by data type. Flag any metric currently collected as attribute that could be captured as continuous — convert it if you can.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 5.** Write a complete operational definition for each metric: what is measured, the instrument, the unit, the sampling point, the collector and the defect criterion.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 6.** Test each operational definition by having two people apply it independently to the same item. If they disagree, the definition is not yet operational.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 7.** Identify your stratification factors — plant, line, shift, operator, product family, supplier. You must capture these at collection time or you can never stratify later.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 8.** Build the data collection plan table: metric, definition, type, source, sample size, frequency, collector, stratification fields and start date.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 9.** Design the check sheet or data form. Make the easy path the correct path, or collectors will improvise.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 10.** Dry-run the plan on a small batch and fix whatever breaks before committing to full collection.

____________________________________________________________________________________

### 7. Self-check

Every capstone metric has a written operational definition that two people apply identically, stratification fields are captured at source, and the plan has survived a dry run.
