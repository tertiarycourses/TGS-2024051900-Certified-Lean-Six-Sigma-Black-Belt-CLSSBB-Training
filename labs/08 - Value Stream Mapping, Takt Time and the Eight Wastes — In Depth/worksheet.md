### 6. Step notes

**Step 1.** Recap the VSM in full: what it shows that a process map does not — inventory, information flow, lead time and the push/pull signal.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 2.** Recap the standard VSM icons: process box, data box, inventory triangle, push arrow, supermarket, truck, information flow and the timeline ladder.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 3.** Map the current state of your capstone value stream from customer order to delivery. Include the information flow that triggers each step.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 4.** Populate each data box: cycle time, changeover time, uptime, batch size, number of operators and first-pass yield.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 5.** Draw the timeline ladder underneath. Put value-added time on the lower rung and waiting/queue time on the upper rung.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 6.** Calculate total lead time, total value-added time, and the value-added ratio (VA time / lead time). Most processes land under 5 percent.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 7.** Calculate takt time = available production time / customer demand for the period. Compare takt against each step's cycle time to find the bottleneck.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 8.** Recap the eight wastes in full (DOWNTIME: Defects, Overproduction, Waiting, Non-utilised talent, Transport, Inventory, Motion, Extra processing).

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 9.** Walk your value stream and log every instance of each waste. Quantify each in time or money — an unquantified waste will not win funding.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 10.** Mark the three points on the VSM where the largest waste concentrates. These become improvement candidates for Day 4.

____________________________________________________________________________________
