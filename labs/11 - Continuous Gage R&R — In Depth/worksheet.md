### 6. Step notes

**Step 1.** Recap the components of measurement variation in full: observed variation = actual process variation + measurement system variation.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 2.** Recap the five MSA properties: accuracy (bias), linearity, stability, repeatability (equipment) and reproducibility (operator).

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 3.** Distinguish repeatability from reproducibility precisely — same operator repeating, versus different operators on the same part.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 4.** Design a crossed Gage R&R for your capstone metric: 10 parts spanning the full process range, 3 operators, 3 repeat measurements each.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 5.** Select parts that span the real range of production. Parts clustered near the mean will flatter the gauge and hide its weakness.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 6.** Randomise the measurement order and blind the operators to previous readings, or you will measure memory rather than the gauge.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 7.** Run the study and record all 90 measurements in the standard crossed layout.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 8.** Calculate %study variation for repeatability, reproducibility and total Gage R&R. Apply the acceptance rule: under 10% acceptable, 10-30% marginal, over 30% unacceptable.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 9.** Calculate the number of distinct categories (ndc). ndc must be 5 or more for the gauge to distinguish parts usefully.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 10.** If the gauge fails, decide the fix: recalibrate, retrain operators, tighten the operational definition or replace the instrument. Then re-run.

____________________________________________________________________________________
