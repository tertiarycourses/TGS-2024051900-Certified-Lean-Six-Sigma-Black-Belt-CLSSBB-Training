### 6. Step notes

**Step 1.** Choose your capstone process. Strongly preferred: a real process from your own workplace where you can obtain data. Fallback: the Meridian Medical Devices scenario.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 2.** List at least five candidate improvement projects within that process or business area. Write each as a PROBLEM, never as a solution.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 3.** Build a project selection matrix. Score each candidate 1-5 on: strategic fit, annualised financial benefit, data availability, scope manageability, sponsor commitment and technical risk.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 4.** Weight the criteria — strategic fit and financial benefit usually carry the highest weight — and compute a weighted total for each candidate.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 5.** Reject any candidate that is a solution in disguise ('deploy a new MES'), has no accessible data, or has no named sponsor. Record WHY each was rejected.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 6.** Select your capstone project — the highest weighted score that survives the rejection filters.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 7.** Estimate the Cost of Poor Quality for the selected project: scrap, rework, warranty, expedite, lost margin and capacity consumed. Annualise it.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 8.** Write the charter: business case, problem statement, goal statement (SMART), scope (in/out), preliminary Y, team, sponsor, milestones and expected benefit.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 9.** Write your problem statement to the four-part test: the process, the time period, the measurable gap and the business impact — with no cause and no solution in it.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 10.** Identify your sponsor and record what you need from them. Where possible, obtain real sign-off before Day 2.

____________________________________________________________________________________

You have a weighted selection matrix with at least five scored candidates, a documented rejection rationale, an annualised COPQ figure, and a complete charter whose problem statement contains no cause and no solution.
