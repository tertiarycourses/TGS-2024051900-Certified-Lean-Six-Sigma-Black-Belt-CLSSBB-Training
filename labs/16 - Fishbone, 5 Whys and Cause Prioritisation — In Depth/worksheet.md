**Step 3.** Write your capstone problem statement precisely at the head of the fishbone. A vague effect produces vague causes.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 4.** Facilitate the fishbone with your team, generating at least 25 candidate causes across all six bones. Thin bones signal blind spots, not absence of causes.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 5.** Recap the 5 Whys and its discipline: continue until the answer is a process or system failure, never a person to blame.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 6.** Apply 5 Whys to your top five fishbone causes. Note where a single chain branches — real causal chains are often trees, not lines.

**Step 9.** Filter the ranked list for TESTABILITY — for each cause, can you state a hypothesis and obtain data? An untestable cause cannot be proven in Analyze.
