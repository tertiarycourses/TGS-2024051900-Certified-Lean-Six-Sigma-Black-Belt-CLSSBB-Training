### 6. Step notes

**Step 1.** Take your ranked proven root causes from Lab 21. Every solution generated in this lab must trace to one of them.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 2.** Recap divergent generation techniques in full: brainstorming, brainwriting (6-3-5), SCAMPER, anti-solution thinking and analogy from other industries.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 3.** Recap benchmarking and its four steps — plan, collect, analyse, adapt. Look outside your industry, where the best analogous processes usually are.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 4.** Generate at least three candidate solutions for each proven root cause. One solution per cause means you have not really explored the space.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 5.** Recap the Lean countermeasure toolkit in full: 5S, poka-yoke, pull systems, JIT, Heijunka (levelling), Jidoka (autonomation) and standard work.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 6.** Recap poka-yoke levels specifically: prevention (error impossible), detection (error caught immediately) and warning (error flagged). Always prefer prevention.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 7.** Map each Lean tool to the root causes in your capstone it could plausibly address, and generate at least two poka-yoke ideas.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 8.** Build the weighted solution selection matrix. Criteria typically: impact on Y, implementation cost, time to benefit, technical risk, ease of adoption and sustainability.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 9.** Weight the criteria with your sponsor, not alone — the weighting reflects business priorities that are theirs to set.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 10.** Score every candidate solution and compute weighted totals.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 11.** Sanity-check the top-ranked solutions: do they collectively address the causes explaining most of the Y gap? If not, revisit generation.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 12.** Identify which solutions have controllable factors suitable for DOE optimisation in Labs 23-25.

____________________________________________________________________________________

### 7. Self-check

Every solution traces to a proven root cause, criteria weights are agreed with the sponsor, and your selected set collectively addresses the majority of the quantified Y gap.
