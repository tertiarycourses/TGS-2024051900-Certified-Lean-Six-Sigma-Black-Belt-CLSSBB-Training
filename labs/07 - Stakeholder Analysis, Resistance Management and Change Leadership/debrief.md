# Trainer Debrief — Lab 7

## Stakeholder Analysis, Resistance Management and Change Leadership

**Case study:** Meridian Medical Devices — seal weld burst pressure  
**Objective:** Analyse stakeholders and plan change leadership for your capstone (A1, K1).

> **TRAINER ONLY.** Do not hand this out before the lab is complete.

---

## Expected analysis

**`lab-07-stakeholder-analysis.xlsx`**

Three stakeholders are both HIGH influence and OPPOSED — the Suzhou Plant Manager, the Manufacturing Engineering Lead and the Suzhou night shift supervisor. They are exactly the people whose process the data has just criticised, so their resistance is predictable and usually political rather than technical: the Lab 15 and Lab 19 findings say their cell is the problem. Treating that as a communication task ('they need to understand the data') is the classic Black Belt failure. The action that moves them is co-ownership — put the Engineering Lead on the DOE team so the fixture fix is HIS, and let the Suzhou manager present the improvement to the steering committee. The Finance Controller must move from Neutral to Supportive before Lab 30, or the benefit will not be signed.

Every dataset is generated from a fixed seed, so these figures are reproducible — re-run the analysis on the shipped workbook and you will get these numbers.

## What good looks like

Every stakeholder is plotted on the influence/support grid, every high-influence resistor has a named action matched to a diagnosed resistance type, and your ADKAR plan covers all five elements.

## Where this sits in the capstone

Black Belt projects fail on people far more often than on statistics. Map influence against support, diagnose resistance by type, and build an ADKAR-based change plan.
