### 6. Step notes

**Step 1.** State precisely why a crossed design is invalid for destructive tests: the part no longer exists to be measured a second time.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 2.** Identify a destructive or non-repeatable measurement in your capstone — or use the Meridian seal-strength test on infusion pump tubing.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 3.** Understand the nested design: each operator measures DIFFERENT parts, and parts are nested within batch rather than crossed with operator.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 4.** Establish homogeneous batches. The design assumes parts within a batch are effectively identical — if they are not, part variation contaminates repeatability.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 5.** Justify batch homogeneity: same lot, same machine, same setup, consecutive production, minimal elapsed time.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 6.** Design the study: 3 operators, 10 batches, 3 parts per operator per batch, all parts within a batch treated as replicates.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 7.** Run the study and record results in the nested layout — operator within batch, not operator crossed with part.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 8.** Compute variance components for repeatability and reproducibility using the nested model. Note that repeatability is now confounded with within-batch part variation.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 9.** State that confounding explicitly in your report. A nested study OVERSTATES repeatability variation, so it is a conservative test.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 10.** Decide accept/reject, and record the design choice and its rationale for the Day 5 presentation — expect this to be challenged.
