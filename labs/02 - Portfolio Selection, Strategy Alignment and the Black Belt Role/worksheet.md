### 6. Step notes

**Step 1.** Write down your organisation's top three business objectives for the current year (or Meridian's: OTIF to 95%, warranty cost down 30%, yield parity across plants).

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 2.** Map each of your five candidate projects from Lab 1 to the objective it serves. Any project that maps to nothing is a candidate for cancellation.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 3.** Build the belt responsibility matrix: White, Yellow, Green, Black, Master Black Belt and Champion. For each, record who leads, who analyses, who mentors and who sponsors.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 4.** List the five things a Black Belt does that a Green Belt does not — lead a portfolio, own advanced statistics, mentor Green Belts, run tollgates, validate benefits with Finance.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 5.** Assess portfolio balance: are all your projects in one phase, one plant or one function? A healthy portfolio is spread across risk and time-to-benefit.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 6.** Identify which of your candidate projects should be delegated to a Green Belt under your mentorship rather than led by you. Record the reasoning.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 7.** Define the tollgate cadence for your capstone: what evidence closes Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve and Control, and who signs each one off.

____________________________________________________________________________________

**Step 8.** Write your Black Belt accountability charter — what you own, what you delegate, what you escalate.
